Faces of the kingdom

  • Elric — The Young King

    This young king who's basically trying to fix everything that went wrong in his kingdom. Guides the player through early stages.

  • Lyra — The Court Mage

    Provides magical assistance and introduces special abilities.

  • Borin — The Builder

    Responsible for reconstruction. Represents progression and upgrades.

  • Mira — The Explorer

    Unlocks new areas and encourages discovery beyond the castle.
